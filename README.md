**Getting to Know the Modeling Industry**
The modeling industry can feel like a lot at first, but understanding it is essential for anyone thinking about a career as a teen model.
Teen models, aged 12 to 17, have a real part to play in promoting products and services, and that runs from fashion through to commercials.
Looking at the different types of modeling, so runway, commercial, and print, helps us work out what interests us and which skills we'll need.
Our parents or guardians have to be part of this with us. They'll back us up, and they'll help us spot scams before we walk into one.
Knowing the legal side of working as a minor matters too, including work permits and the regulations, since that's what keeps us informed and safe.
All of that knowledge is the foundation a modeling career gets built on.

**Building Your Own Brand**
So how do we make our mark in modeling? Building our own brand is essential if we want to stand out. We have to lean into being individuals and put our real selves forward. Companies these days want models who look like actual teenagers rather than adults.
Here are some key steps to help us define our brand:
Identify what makes us unique, like our style or personality.
Experiment with various outfits to find what feels right.
Showcase our genuine preferences in photos.
Stay true to ourselves while being open to feedback.
Remember that our unique look, whether it's curly hair or braces, can be an asset!

**Creating an Impressive Portfolio**
Our brand is the foundation the portfolio gets built on, and the portfolio is our calling card in modeling. Quality images are where it starts, with headshots and full-body shots that show the look that's ours.
If professional photography isn't on the cards, well-lit photos taken at home will do the job, as long as they catch our personality and our style. Updating the portfolio regularly keeps it current and puts our range on show.
Practicing different poses and expressions is how we work out our personal style, and then that style goes into the photos. Keep in mind that quality beats quantity. Five amazing photos say far more than a hundred mediocre ones.
Our portfolio is essential for getting the attention of agencies and casting agents.
